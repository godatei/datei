> **"Datei" is German for _file_.**
>
> But also for document. And dataset. Basically anything you really don't want
> to lose.

Datei is an open source, self-hosted document management platform.

The source code is available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/godatei/datei).

## Why Datei exists

Datei gives you full control over your files. You run the platform on your own
infrastructure, or at least own the whole instance that holds your data: a
storage bucket that is yours and a database no one else shares.

Owning every part of the stack is what keeps you in control of the data you
collect.

Read our [introduction blog post](/blog/introducing-datei/) to learn where
we're headed. Follow the [roadmap](/roadmap/) to see what's next, and sign up
for our [newsletter](/newsletter/) to get early access.
